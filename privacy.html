Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 Nov 2026

This policy explains how Sarvadhara Travels, a unit of SarvadharaSai Enterprises (a sole proprietorship), collects, uses, shares and protects personal information. In this policy, “Sarvadhara Travels”, “we”, “us” and “the agency” mean Sarvadhara Travels (SarvadharaSai Enterprises), Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

1. Who we are

Sarvadhara Travels is the trade name of a unit of SarvadharaSai Enterprises, a sole proprietorship based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. We act as a booking agent and facilitator for travel and related services, including flights (booked through airlines and our consolidator platform, TBO), hotels and other travel services, and we also facilitate courier, cargo and trade services under SarvadharaSai Enterprises.

GSTIN: 33ARVPY1906F1ZF. Website: sarvadharatravels.in.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to take an enquiry, make or manage a booking, take payment, issue tickets or invoices, and meet legal requirements. This may include:

  • Name, phone number, WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Passenger or guest details, including names as they appear on a passport or other ID, date of birth, gender, and passport or ID numbers and expiry dates, when required for a booking.
  • Travel or service details, such as dates, sectors, destinations, hotel stay dates, and special requests you give us (for example a meal or accessibility request).
  • Billing and payment information needed to receive or refund a payment. Card and UPI details entered on a payment gateway are collected by that gateway, not stored by us (see section 5).
  • Booking references, ticket numbers, invoices and records of your messages with us.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type and pages visited, when you use our website, including data collected through cookies as described below.

If you give us details of other passengers or guests, you confirm that they have agreed to this, or that you are otherwise authorised to provide their information.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotes.
  • Make, change, cancel or support bookings with airlines, hotels and other suppliers.
  • Issue tickets, vouchers, invoices and booking confirmations, and to process payments and refunds.
  • Contact you about a booking, a schedule change, or a request you have raised.
  • Comply with law, including tax, accounting, and any request from a competent authority.
  • Improve our website and services, and protect against fraud or misuse.

We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, and other applicable Indian law.

4. Sharing of information

To complete a booking we must share relevant details with the airline, hotel, consolidator (including TBO), payment gateway, and any other supplier needed for that service. We may also share information with government or regulatory authorities, immigration or customs, and with professional advisers, where the law requires or allows it.

Passenger and guest data may be transferred outside India where the airline, hotel or other supplier, or the country of travel, requires it to provide the service. We share only what is needed for that purpose.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Payments

When you pay online, the payment is processed by our payment service provider (the payment gateway). Card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs and similar payment secrets are entered on the gateway’s own secure pages and are not stored on our website. The gateway may keep the records it needs to process the payment, prevent fraud and meet its legal duties, under its own privacy policy.

We may receive a confirmation of payment, a transaction reference, the payment method used, and the amount, so that we can confirm your booking and, if needed, process a refund to the original payment method.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar tools that are needed for the site to work, to remember a language preference, or to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not work properly if cookies are blocked. If we use a third-party tool (for example a language tool), that provider may set its own cookies under its policy.

7. Retention

We keep booking, invoice and communication records for as long as needed to provide the service, handle refunds or claims, and meet tax, accounting and other legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it where reasonably practicable.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct or update the personal information we hold about you, or to erase it where we no longer need it and the law does not require us to keep it. To make a request, use the contact details in section 13. We may need to verify your identity before we respond. Some records must be retained for legal or accounting reasons even if a deletion is requested.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Our services are not directed at children. We may hold a child’s travel details only when an adult provides them to complete a booking for that child. The person who gives us those details is responsible for being authorised to do so.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. The policy in force when you use the website or make a booking is the one that applies.

12. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Chennai, Tamil Nadu have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to any rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
