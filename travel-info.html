Guidance

What to check before travel

Rules change. Always confirm with official government and airline sources.

Passport validity

  • Many countries ask for at least 6 months’ validity beyond your stay.
  • Blank pages are often required for visas and entry stamps.
  • Name on the ticket must match the passport exactly.
  • Damaged passports can lead to denied boarding — replace early if needed.

Visas

Visa rules depend on your nationality and destination. Check official portals:

  • Indian e-Visa / visa information (indianvisaonline.gov.in)
  • UAE ICP (icp.gov.ae) — also see u.ae visa guidance
  • For other countries, use the embassy or consulate website of your destination.

Sarvadhara Travels can assist with forms and appointments — we are not a government office.

Health & vaccination

  • Some destinations require yellow fever or other certificates.
  • Airlines and countries may update health screening without notice.
  • Carry prescriptions and a doctor’s letter for medicines you travel with.
  • Confirm current advice on the IATA Travel Centre and your destination’s health ministry site.

Baggage & airline rules

  • Cabin and checked allowances follow the operating airline’s fare rules.
  • Extra baggage, seats and meals are charged by the airline.
  • Check the airline’s official website for size, weight and restricted items.
  • Examples: Emirates, Air India, IndiGo, Qatar Airways — always verify on the carrier you fly.

Information is for guidance only. Entry rules are set by governments and airlines and may change.
