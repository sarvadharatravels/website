Trade FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who handles export and import?

SarvadharaSai Enterprises, a Sarvadhara group business, handles trade. Travel services remain under SARVADHARA TRAVELS.

Do you hold an IEC?

Yes — Importer–Exporter Code (IEC) is held. Other registrations (Spices Board, FSSAI, APEDA and similar) are complied with as applicable for each shipment; we do not claim certifications we do not hold.

Can you arrange samples?

Yes, sampling can be arranged where applicable before a full order.

Do you publish prices online?

No. Pricing depends on product grade, quantity, packing, Incoterm and market conditions. Send a trade enquiry for a quotation.

Can you help with import sourcing into India?

Yes — import sourcing is available on request. Share the product and requirements and we will advise.
