Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 11 Nov 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work when you book through Sarvadhara Travels, a unit of SarvadharaSai Enterprises (a sole proprietorship), Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. It should be read with our Terms and Conditions.

1. Our role 2. How to request a cancellation 3. Flight bookings 4. Hotel bookings 5. Other services 6. Agency service fee 7. How and when refunds are paid 8. Changes to a booking 9. Courier and cargo 10. Governing law 11. Contact

1. Our role

Sarvadhara Travels acts as a booking agent and facilitator. Flights are booked through the airline and our consolidator platform (TBO). Hotels and other travel services are booked with the relevant hotel or supplier. The contract of carriage or stay is between you and the airline, hotel or other supplier. Cancellation, no-show, date change and refund eligibility are decided by that supplier’s fare rules, rate rules and policies, which we will apply when we process your request.

2. How to request a cancellation or refund

Please contact us as soon as you wish to cancel or change a booking, and before the departure or check-in time. A delay in telling us can reduce what, if anything, can be refunded under the supplier’s rules (for example after a no-show).

You can request a cancellation or refund by:

Please include the booking or ticket reference, passenger or guest names, travel or stay dates, and whether you are asking for a cancellation, a refund, or a change. We may also need the original payment details so that any approved refund can be sent by the original payment method.

3. Flight bookings

Flight cancellations and refunds follow the operating airline’s conditions of carriage and the fare rules of the ticket purchased. Some fares are non-refundable; a non-refundable fare remains non-refundable, except to the extent the airline itself allows a refund (for example of certain taxes, or as required by applicable law or a government or airline directive).

Airline, airport and consolidator cancellation charges, penalties, fare differences and any non-refundable taxes or fees charged by the airline or TBO are payable by the customer and will be deducted from any refund.

We can process a refund only after, and only to the extent that, the airline (through TBO, where applicable) actually refunds the amount to us. We will follow up with the supplier on your behalf, but we do not control and cannot guarantee the supplier’s processing time.

4. Hotel bookings

Hotel cancellations and refunds follow the hotel’s or lodging supplier’s rate rules and cancellation policy for the rate you booked. These may include free-cancellation windows, charges for late cancellation, or no refund for a no-show or a non-refundable rate. Any cancellation charge or amount withheld by the hotel or supplier will be deducted from the refund.

We process a hotel refund only after, and only to the extent that, the hotel or supplier actually refunds the amount to us.

5. Other travel services

For holidays, tours, car rentals, transfers and similar services, cancellation and refund terms are those of the supplier and as confirmed to you at the time of booking. Government fees and charges for passport or visa applications are subject to the rules of the issuing authority and are often non-refundable once paid. We will tell you what the supplier or authority has confirmed in respect of your request.

6. Agency service fee

Any refund is of the amount actually returned to us by the airline, hotel or other supplier, minus the agency service fee for that booking, except where Indian law requires otherwise. The service fee is the agency’s charge for arranging the booking and is separate from the fare or rate, and from airline, hotel or supplier charges. The applicable service fee is confirmed at the time of booking and on the invoice; this policy does not set a fixed amount.

7. How and when refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid only to the original payer, and by the original payment method wherever that is possible (including through the payment gateway used for an online payment).

Once the airline, hotel or other supplier has credited the refund to us, we aim to send the refundable amount to you within 5 to 7 working days. The supplier’s own refund timeline is in addition to this period and varies by airline, hotel, consolidator and payment method. Bank or payment-gateway processing time after we release the refund may also apply.

We will share the supplier’s refund reference with you when we have it. If a refund cannot be sent by the original method, we will contact you to arrange another reasonable method.

8. Changes to a booking

Date, name, routing or guest changes are subject to the airline’s, hotel’s or other supplier’s rules and availability. Supplier change fees, fare or rate differences, and taxes are payable by the customer. A change request should be made through the same contact channels listed in section 2.

Passenger names must be entered exactly as on the passport or government-issued travel ID. Before booking a ticket, the customer must check the airline’s policy on long surnames and first names, including single-name or surname-only passengers, name length limits, and how titles or middle names are entered. Name corrections, if the airline allows them, are subject to airline fees and the agency’s service charges. See Passenger Names in our Terms and Conditions.

9. Courier and cargo

Cancellation and refunds for courier, cargo, import or export bookings follow the carrier’s and platform’s rules, as set out in section 15 of our Terms and Conditions.

10. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. The courts at Chennai, Tamil Nadu have exclusive jurisdiction. Statutory passenger rights against an airline (for example under DGCA rules) remain rights against the airline; we will assist you in raising such a request with the airline.
